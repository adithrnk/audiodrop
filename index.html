Good to know

Can I paste a YouTube or Instagram link?

This static version does not extract audio from social-media pages. Use the platform’s official download options or a creator-provided download link, where available. Direct media-file links may work if the server permits browser access.

Is 320 kbps always better?

It creates a larger MP3, but it cannot restore detail that is absent from the source. Choose a quality that fits the original recording and your needs.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

Local file conversion is designed to run in your browser. Direct URL downloads are fetched by your browser and may fail if the source blocks cross-origin access (CORS).
